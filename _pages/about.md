I am Zhiwei Huang, a PhD candidate at EPFL, currently working at the intersection of MR physics, signal processing, and system-level imaging engineering.

My expertise spans pulse sequence development, non-Cartesian trajectory implementation, image reconstruction, and quantitative signal processing. I am interested in building robust and reproducible MR acquisition–reconstruction pipelines that translate theoretical models into deployable imaging solutions (e.g., [representative work](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1002/hbm.70176)).

### Technical Focus
- Pulse sequence programming  
- Non-Cartesian trajectory implementation  
- MR image reconstruction  
- MRSI / Diffusion MRS signal postprocessing  
- End-to-end imaging pipeline development and validation  

### Research Interests
Advanced MR methodology, image reconstruction, signal postprocessing, and system-level optimization of acquisition and reconstruction.
